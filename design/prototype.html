Borrow what you need. Lend what you don't use.

Most of what we own sits in a closet, and most of what we need, we need once. Lendbox connects people who have things with people who need them, nearby and by the date.

Things sit idle

A drill gets used for a few minutes at a time. A tent comes out once a summer. A keyboard waits between gigs.

Borrowing is messy

Today it runs on Facebook groups, WhatsApp chats and shared spreadsheets: posts wait for admins, lists go stale, and nobody knows what is free on which day.

Lendbox fixes the how

One listing with clear terms, a live calendar instead of "still available?", and a request that carries the dates.

How it works

No payments through the site. You agree on price and deposit, and meet in person.

  1. Find it

    Search by what, when and where, or browse a community.

  2. Request your dates

    See the price and deposit up front. The owner approves with one tap.

  3. Meet, use, return, review

    Both sides review each other. Reviews show only after both are in.
