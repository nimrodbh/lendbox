Community guidelines

Show your item honestly. Agree on the money and meet somewhere you both feel safe. Bring things back on time and treat the other person as a neighbor.

Show what you really have

Use real photos of your item and describe its actual condition. Mention wear, missing parts and anything a borrower needs to know. A small fault is easier to discuss before pickup than after it.

Keep unsafe and prohibited items off Lendbox. That includes vehicles of any kind, weapons, medication, child car seats, live animals, food, anything illegal to own or lend in Israel, and items that need a license or insurance to lend.

Keep deposits fair and clear

Choose a deposit that is fair for the item's value and condition. Every loan needs one, even when the daily price is free. Agree on the total price, when the deposit comes back and how you would handle damage or late return.

Money changes hands in person at pickup. Lendbox never charges, holds or refunds it. Write down what you both agree so neither person has to rely on memory.

Give people an answer

Reply when you can, even if the answer is no. A request closes after 48 hours without an answer. Approval automatically declines other overlapping requests, so approve only when you are ready to lend.

If your plans change, tell the other person promptly. A quick message can save a wasted trip.

Meet somewhere you feel comfortable

Choose a public or semi-public spot, such as a cafe entrance, campus gate or building lobby. Prefer daylight and tell someone where you are going. You can step away if anything feels wrong.

Keep the exact pickup spot in the private pickup field, not in public text or photos. It is shared with the borrower after approval. Check the item and its parts together, and ask for a quick demonstration if needed. The owner marks pickup.

Make the next loan easy

Return things on time, in the same state, with all their parts. Clean and dry them as agreed before packing them away. Tell the owner about damage or a delay as soon as you know. The owner marks the return.

Be kind and respect privacy

Keep messages clear, patient and about the loan. No threats, harassment or pressure. Booking messages are visible only to the two sides, but that is no reason to share passwords or unnecessary private details.

Talk first, then report

If it is safe, start by telling the other person what happened and what would help. Give them a chance to reply. If you cannot resolve it, or the behavior breaks the rules, report it. You do not need to contact someone who makes you feel unsafe.

Admins can see reports and hide listings or users. A report does not arrange a refund or return a deposit. If someone is in immediate danger, seek urgent local help rather than waiting for a report to be read.

Leave an honest review

After the return, describe your own experience fairly. Do not trade ratings, threaten a bad review or post private details. Reviews become visible when both sides have reviewed, or 14 days after the return.
